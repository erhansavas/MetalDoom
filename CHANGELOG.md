# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-25)

First release.

- The original DOOM engine, native on Apple silicon Macs, drawn with Metal at
  the display's full resolution and frame rate (60 fps on most Macs, 120 fps
  on ProMotion displays)
- Adaptive resolution that keeps the frame rate under load
- Gore: blood, splatter, drips, footprints, fragments, hit reactions with
  physics and its own sounds, spread over every CPU core
- Game controllers: DualSense haptics and adaptive triggers per weapon,
  Xbox, DualShock and others; the controller used last plays
- Opens on its menu; Skip Transitions; status bar card; Command-Q asks first
